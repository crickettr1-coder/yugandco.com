WORKING QUESTIONS

Clarity before
commitment.

Do you publish fixed packages?+

The website explains engagement types rather than fixed-price packages. A proposal follows a review of your requirements, existing assets and delivery needs.

Can our internal team stay involved?+

Yes. Review contacts, access, content responsibilities and handover requirements are agreed so the work fits your team.

What happens if the scope changes?+

Changes to deliverables, dependencies or priorities are reviewed before additional work is committed. Any fee or timing impact should be agreed.

Will we receive account access and files?+

Account ownership, working files, final formats, licensing and handover requirements are clarified in the proposal.
