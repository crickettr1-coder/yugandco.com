# Yug & Co private content checklist

This file is not copied into `dist/` and must not be uploaded to the public document root.

## Needed before publishing reviews

- One approved testimonial quote, exactly as approved.
- Person’s name, role, company and service provided.
- Permission to publish the quote and any date/source link.
- Permission and source details for any portrait or company mark.

## Business details still to confirm

- Public enquiry inbox and verified sender address.
- Public business phone number, if one should be shown.
- Legal business name, address and governing-law details for final website terms.
- Privacy contact and retention details.
- Analytics consent approach and regional requirements.

## Production delivery

- Hostinger PHP version and enabled extensions.
- Hostinger environment-variable loading method.
- SMTP host, port, encryption, username and password.
- A real inbox-delivery test after deployment.

## Content boundaries

- No client names, logos, results, awards, certifications or founder biography are published until verified.
- Portfolio entries are independent concepts and remain labelled as such.
