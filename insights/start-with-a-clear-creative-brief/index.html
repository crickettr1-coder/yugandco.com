Content & Creative

A stronger campaign starts with a clearer brief.

A brief is a shared decision about what the work needs to do. It gives the creative room to be distinctive without losing sight of the business.

Start with one useful objective

Define the action you want someone to take: understand an offer, enquire about a service or explore a product. A campaign with several competing objectives often produces a message that is difficult to follow.

Describe the audience’s situation

Go beyond a broad demographic. What has prompted the person to look? What do they already know? What is preventing them from taking the next step? These questions help the work become more relevant.

Make the offer and evidence clear

State what you are offering, why it matters and which verified details support it. Share real product information and approved claims. A distinctive visual cannot compensate for an unclear offer.

Agree the practical boundaries

List channels, formats, budget, timing, approvers and deliverables. Separate requirements from preferences. A shared brief makes feedback more specific and helps everyone assess an idea against the same goal.
