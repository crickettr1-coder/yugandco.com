Practical marketing guide

In this guideBuild themes around real questionsKeep a recognisable voiceMatch the format to the ideaLearn from the right signals
01

Build themes around real questions

Collect the questions people ask before buying or enquiring. Explain your process, clarify your offer and show relevant details. Useful themes make the content easier to plan and more meaningful to the audience.

02

Keep a recognisable voice

Decide how your brand sounds, which words it uses and what it should avoid. Share examples with everyone involved in writing and reviewing content so the voice stays consistent.

03

Match the format to the idea

A short demonstration, a photo sequence and a written explanation each do different work. Choose a format because it helps communicate the idea, then adapt it for the platform.

04

Learn from the right signals

Engagement is context rather than a complete business result. Look at saves, meaningful questions, website visits and enquiry quality where those measures are relevant. Use what you learn to improve the next cycle.
