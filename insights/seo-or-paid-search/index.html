Search & Strategy

SEO or paid search? Start with the business goal.

SEO and paid search reach people in different ways. The right starting point depends on your timeframe, website, offer and resources.

01

When a focused paid campaign can help

Paid search can put a relevant offer in front of people searching for it. It needs an agreed media budget, a useful landing page and reliable conversion tracking. Test with a defined question rather than assuming every click is valuable.

02

When to invest in organic foundations

SEO strengthens the usefulness and discoverability of your website over time. Technical issues, unclear service pages and missing content can prevent people from finding or understanding your business. These foundations can support other channels too.

03

Look at the journey after the click

Both channels depend on what happens on the website. Explain the offer, provide relevant information and make the next step easy. Check the form works and define what qualifies as a useful enquiry.

04

Choose based on evidence

Review existing demand, your website’s condition and your available budget. Agree on a reporting period and measure the business action you care about. A combined plan may be useful, but neither channel guarantees a result.
