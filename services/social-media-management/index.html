SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Which platforms do you manage?+

We choose platforms around your audience and resources. The proposal names the channels, publishing frequency and deliverables.

Do you reply to messages and comments?+

Community management is scoped separately, with clear response times, escalation rules and approval boundaries.

Do we need to be on every platform?+

No. The right channels depend on your audience, content resources and business goals. A manageable presence is more useful than an unsupported channel list.

Can we provide our own photos and videos?+

Yes. Asset quality, usage rights, file requirements and the review process are agreed before production.
