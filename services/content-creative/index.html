SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Can you work with our existing identity?+

Yes. We can develop work within your current guidelines or recommend specific improvements where the brief requires them.

How many revisions are included?+

Review stages, revision rounds and final file formats are agreed in the scope before production starts.

Does this include a complete rebrand?+

A full identity project is different from campaign creative. We define the exact identity, copy and production deliverables in the scope.

Who approves the final assets?+

The proposal names the approver, review stages and final sign-off requirements so feedback stays focused.
