SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Is this a one-off project?+

A strategy can be a standalone engagement. Implementation and ongoing support are separate decisions, with the scope agreed in advance.

Can we use the plan with our own team?+

Yes. Handover requirements, working documents and any implementation support are defined in the proposal.

Do we need to invest in every channel?+

No. A strategy should identify what to prioritise and what to defer, based on the business context and available resources.

What if our goals change?+

A useful plan includes review points. Material changes to the business, offer or budget may require a revised scope or roadmap.
