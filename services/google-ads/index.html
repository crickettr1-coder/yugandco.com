SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Is advertising spend included in your fee?+

Media spend and management fees are separate. Both are agreed before work begins, along with any landing-page or tracking work.

Can you improve an existing account?+

We can start by reviewing campaign structure, conversion tracking, search terms and landing-page relevance before recommending changes.

Will clicks immediately become customers?+

Not necessarily. Your offer, landing page, competition, sales response and budget all affect the outcome.

Who owns the advertising account?+

Account access, billing and handover are agreed before work begins. Ownership and responsibilities should remain clear throughout the engagement.
